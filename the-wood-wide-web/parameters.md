**Title:** *The Wood Wide Web*

**Summary:** mycorrhizal networks again — trees sharing resources and warnings through fungal threads

**Sound instructions:** Organic, grounded, and luminous. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar and warm strings (cello, violin) layered with subtle environmental textures—gentle wind through leaves, soft soil rustling, the breathing quality of earth itself. Underneath, soft warm synth pads to suggest the vast hidden networks. Patient and contemplative, slightly mystical but genuine and alive.

**BPM:** 75
**Length:** 3.5 minutes
