**Title:** *The Sun Is a Rumor*

**Summary:** hydrothermal vent ecosystems living on sulfur chemistry, needing no sunlight at all

**Sound instructions:** Slow, oceanic, patient. Cinematic trip-hop meets post-rock. Open on deep sub-bass drone and a sparse, wet piano figure with lots of reverb tail — feels submerged, muffled, like sound traveling through water. Female vocal, low and breathy, close-mic'd and intimate against the huge space. Verse 1: almost no percussion, just soft mallet hits and the drone. First chorus: a slow heartbeat kick enters, warm analog bass. Verse 2: add faint bowed cello and a distant metallic shimmer. Bridge strips almost everything away to voice and a single sustained note, then builds — layered vocal harmonies stacking, tremolo guitar swelling in. Final chorus is full and enormous: live drums, strings, choral backing vocals, but never harsh — it should feel like warmth blooming in cold water, not a rock climax. Outro decays back into the drone and one last piano note. Mix warm, dark, wide.
