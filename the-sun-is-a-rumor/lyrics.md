[Verse 1]
Two miles down, and the pressure is a hand
holding everything in place
No dawn ever broke here, no shadow ever ran
across a single face
They told us light's the price of being alive,
that everything green owes a debt —
but there's a garden in the dark and it's doing fine,
and it has never paid it yet

[Chorus]
The sun is a rumor,
a story that the surface tells
Down here we make our own
out of sulfur and the heat of the shells
We are not waiting
on a morning that was never going to come
The sun is a rumor —
and we were never the ones who needed one

[Verse 2]
Tube worms in a red bloom, standing six feet tall,
no mouth, no gut, no eyes,
drinking down the poison pouring off the wall
of a chimney built from iron and time
They said you need a star to make a living thing,
some furnace far away
We found the furnace closer. We found it underneath.
And we never saw the day.

[Bridge]
And when the vent goes cold — because they always do —
the whole town packs its bones,
lets go, and rides a current with no map, no crew,
out looking for another stone
Somebody always finds it
Somebody always does
Somebody always finds it

[Final Chorus]
The sun is a rumor,
so is the ending, so is the loss
Down here we make our own
out of sulfur and the pressure and the dark
We are not waiting.
We were never the ones who needed one.
