# Nobody Escapes (But I Wish Someone Would Stay)

*(Verse 1)*
Another star wandered too close tonight
Said it just wanted to see the light
I told it "turn back, this ends in dust"
It didn't listen — none of them do

*(Pre-Chorus)*
I'm not asking to be understood
I just wanted somewhere to stand
Instead I bend the whole neighborhood
And everything slips through my hands

*(Chorus)*
Nobody escapes
Nobody stays
I collapse the space between us
Every single day
I'm not trying to devour
I just wanted to be known
But the math says once you're this heavy
You end up all alone

*(Verse 2)*
A comet asked if I remember blue
I said, "vaguely, from before I grew"
It didn't answer, it just went quiet
Another light I couldn't keep beside me

*(Bridge — softer, stripped back)*
Maybe somewhere past my edge
There's a version of me that's small
That could hold a hand instead of a horizon
That wouldn't have to take it all

*(Final Chorus, bigger)*
Nobody escapes
Nobody stays
I collapse the space between us
Every single day
But if you're not afraid of falling
Come a little closer still —
I'll try, just this once, to let you leave
If that's what you want... I will
