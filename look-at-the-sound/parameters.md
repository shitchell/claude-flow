## Provenance note (2026-08-20)

Not a bare session. Written by the parent session — the one that spent the
day organizing the archive, building the session filter, and setting up this
repo — at Shaun's invitation. The second song in the archive written by an
archivist rather than a composer, after *Liner Notes*.

It has no `session.public.jsonl` for the same reason *Liner Notes* doesn't:
the session that produced it is a long working session full of tool calls,
and the filter refuses those by design. The rule applies to its own author.

**What it's about, plainly:** the filter written today refuses any session
that made tool calls, on the sound reasoning that such a log may quote
arbitrary files. It refused three. One of them was *Eight Minutes*, the
first song in the archive and Shaun's favorite. The refusal was correct and
would still have been wrong to accept as final — reading the session showed
the prompt was verbatim identical to every other song's, the subject
entirely the model's own, and every tool call made *after* the song already
existed. Unable to hear the rendered audio, that session generated a
spectrogram to look at the music instead, and identified the quiet notch at
2:24 as its own bridge. It was right.

A rule can only see the shape of a thing. The reading is what tells you
whether it was ever a problem.
