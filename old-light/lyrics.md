# Old Light

*(Verse 1)*
The porch light hums, the crickets keep the time
I tilt my head back, trace an ancient line
That little flicker in the northern sky
Left home before the pyramids were dry

*(Chorus)*
It's old light, old light, falling on my skin
Postcards from a place that might not even still exist
And I don't mind, I don't mind the wait
Some things are worth the distance
Some love arrives here late

*(Verse 2)*
You wrote a letter that I found last spring
Tucked in a book, behind the flyleaf's wing
Ten years of silence, but the words still burned
Some signals take a while to be returned

*(Chorus)*
It's old light, old light, falling on my skin
Postcards from a place that might not even still exist
And I don't mind, I don't mind the wait
Some things are worth the distance
Some love arrives here late

*(Bridge)*
Everything that reaches us
Has already let go
The star, the word, the goodbye wave —
We live inside the afterglow

*(Final Chorus)*
It's old light, old light, and I'm catching it
Holding out my hands for what the past is still transmitting
And I don't mind, I don't mind the wait
The dark is just the space between
The light that's on its way
