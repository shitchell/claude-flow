**Title:** *Old Light*

**Summary:** starlight from long-dead stars still arriving, like letters and love that show up late

**Sound instructions:** Warm, intimate indie-folk with an ambient edge. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar as the backbone, soft brushed drums entering at the first chorus, and slow-swelling analog synth pads that shimmer underneath like distant stars. Gentle tape hiss and a slightly lo-fi warmth throughout. Vocals soft and close-mic'd, almost whispered in the verses, opening up with airy layered harmonies in the choruses. The bridge should strip down to just voice and a single sustained pad, then bloom back in for the final chorus with subtle glockenspiel accents.

**BPM:** 76
**Length:** 3:45
