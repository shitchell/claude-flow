**Title:** *Midnight Zone*

**Summary:** bioluminescent life in the lightless deep ocean, glowing for no audience

**Sound instructions:** Slow, spacious dream-folk / slowcore. Fingerpicked clean electric guitar drenched in long reverb, upright bass, brushed drums that don't enter until the second verse. Bowed cello swells underneath. Faint analog synth pads that ping and decay like distant sonar. Vocals close-mic'd and breathy — intimate, almost spoken, doubled and stacked only on the chorus. Keep it *sparse*; let silence do real work. One slow build into the bridge that gets full and wide but never loud, then strip back to almost nothing for the final line. Underwater, patient, warm-in-the-cold.

**BPM:** 68
**Length:** 3:40
**Seed:** 4000 *(the depth in meters where the midnight zone bottoms out)*
