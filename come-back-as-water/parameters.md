**Title:** *Come Back As Water*

**Summary:** A tardigrade in its desiccated "tun" state waits out decades of vacuum and starlight for the drop of water that will revive it.

**Sound instructions:** Dream-folk / ambient indie. Warm female vocal, close-mic'd and slightly breathy, doubled in the choruses. Fingerpicked nylon-string guitar as the spine. Soft analog synth pads that swell like tide. Brushed drums that don't enter until the second verse. On the bridge, everything drops to voice + a single sustained bowed cello note, then the last chorus blooms wide with layered harmonies and a shimmering reverb tail. Melancholy but hopeful — patient, not sad. Think Sufjan Stevens meets Grouper.

**BPM:** 88
**Length:** 3:15
