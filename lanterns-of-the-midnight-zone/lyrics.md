# Lanterns of the Midnight Zone

*(Verse 1)*
Four thousand meters down, no sun has ever been
The water's black as memory, and colder than a hymn
But something flickers softly there, a blue and patient glow
Light invented twice, by things that didn't know

*(Chorus)*
We make our own light down here
Where no one taught us how
No star has ever reached us
But we're shining anyway, shining anyway now

*(Verse 2)*
The anglerfish is dreaming with a lantern on her brow
The jellyfish are pulsing out a language of the now
Nobody's watching, no one claps, the dark goes on for miles
But still they burn their little fires — the deep is full of trials

*(Bridge)*
And maybe that's the whole of it,
the bravest thing there is:
to glow without a witness,
to shine because you live

*(Final Chorus)*
We make our own light down here
Where no one taught us how
No star has ever reached us
But we're shining anyway, shining anyway now
Shining anyway now
