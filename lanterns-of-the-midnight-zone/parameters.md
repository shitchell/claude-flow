**Title:** *Lanterns of the Midnight Zone*

**Summary:** deep-sea creatures in the midnight zone making their own light with no one watching

**Sound instructions:** Dreamy, submerged indie-electronic. Warm analog synth pads with a slow filter sweep, soft female or androgynous vocals with gentle reverb (like singing underwater), sparse sub-bass pulses like whale heartbeats, glassy bell/celesta accents on the chorus, and a subtle field-recording texture of water and hydrophone crackle underneath. Builds from near-silence to a lush, glowing final chorus.

**BPM:** 72
**Length:** ~3:30
**Seed:** 3800 (the average depth of the ocean in meters 🌊)
