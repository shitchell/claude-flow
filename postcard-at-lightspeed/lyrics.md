# Postcard at Lightspeed

*(Verse 1)*
They pressed our voices into gold,
a greeting in a hundred tongues,
whale song, rainfall, Chuck Berry's hands,
the heartbeat of a woman in love.

*(Pre-chorus)*
Strapped it to a hull of foil and hope,
lit a fire underneath the dark—

*(Chorus)*
Now it's forty thousand years to the nearest star,
a postcard at lightspeed, we've gotten this far.
If nobody finds it, that's alright somehow—
we said *we were here*, and we're saying it now.

*(Verse 2)*
Past the planets, past the wind
the sun can throw, past every map,
it doesn't slow, it doesn't grieve,
it just carries the music on its back.

*(Bridge — half-spoken, over static)*
Billions of years from tonight,
when the mountains here have worn to sand,
something might hold it to the light
and hear rain that fell on a world called Earth.

*(Final chorus — big and open)*
It's forty thousand years to the nearest star,
a postcard at lightspeed, look how far we are.
If nobody ever writes back to us now—
we sang that *we were here*. We're still singing it now.
