**Title:** *Rot Is Generous*

**Summary:** a nurse log — a fallen tree feeding beetles, fungus, and a row of new hemlocks as it rots

**Sound instructions:** Swamp gospel stomp. Foot-stomps and handclaps doing most of the percussion, resonator slide guitar, upright bass, a low sawing fiddle drone, tambourine on the backbeat. Gritty, unpolished, close-mic'd — dirt under the fingernails. Lead vocal warm and cracked; big ragged gang vocals on the chorus, like a whole porch full of people. Dry, minimal reverb. Builds to a shouted final chorus, then drops out to just stomps and voices at the end.

**BPM:** 108
**Length:** ~3:30
**Seed:** 3141
