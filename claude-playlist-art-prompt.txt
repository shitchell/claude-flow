A single stenciled handprint on damp cave limestone, made the paleolithic
way: ochre blown around a spread hand, so the hand itself is bare negative
space. The bare stone inside the handprint is not empty — it holds a slow
drift of tiny cold blue-green lights, like deep-sea bioluminescence or
fireflies caught mid-flash, a few of them escaping past the fingertips into
the dark around it. Warm red-ochre pigment against deep blue-black shadow,
one faint warm light source low and to the left. Photographic, shallow depth
of field, fine mineral grain in the rock. Square composition, hand slightly
off-center, generous dark negative space. No text, no letters, no symbols.
