**Title:** *Hundred-Year Bread*

**Summary:** a century-old sourdough starter — domestic immortality fed daily in exchange for bread

**Sound instructions:** Kitchen-floor folk stomp. Boot thumps on floorboards, handclaps, upright bass, fiddle, wheezy accordion, a little clawhammer banjo. Warm close-miked lead vocal, slightly cracked and conversational — sounds like someone telling you this over a counter, not performing it. Chorus opens into loose, unpolished gang vocals, four or five people who aren't all in tune. Bridge drops to voice and bass almost bare, then the whole room comes back in for the last chorus. Outro is a fading round of gang vocals over stomps and claps, like the band walking out of the room still singing.

**BPM:** 112
**Length:** 200s
**Seed:** 1919
