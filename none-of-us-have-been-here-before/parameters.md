**Title:** *None of Us Have Been Here Before*

**Summary:** monarch migration — a journey no single butterfly completes, finished by great-great-grandchildren

**Sound instructions:** Warm indie-folk, unhurried. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar as the throughline, joined gradually by upright bass, brushed drums, and strings that swell in on the choruses like wind picking up under a wing. Female or androgynous vocal, breathy and warm, not belted — more like someone thinking out loud. Building arrangement — sparse verses, fuller choruses, big open bridge, then it thins back out at the end to just voice and guitar, like the flight settling.

**BPM:** 96
**Length:** 3:40
