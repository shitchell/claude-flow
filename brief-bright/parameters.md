**Title:** *Brief Bright*

**Summary:** fireflies — one summer season of flashing alone in the dark to be found, as their numbers decline

**Sound instructions:** Ethereal and pulsing—shimmery synth bells or tones that echo firefly flashing, grounded with acoustic guitar or warm strings. Melancholic but urgent, wondering but fragile. Vocals emotional and questioning. Build gently through verses and choruses, peak at the bridge, fade into the outro like dusk becoming night.

**BPM:** 108
**Length:** ~3:30 or whatever flows naturally
