**Title:** *One Loud Summer*

**Summary:** periodical cicadas — seventeen years underground for one loud summer of song

**Sound instructions:** Starts as sparse, patient indie-folk — muted fingerpicked guitar, a low sustained drone underneath (like something humming underground). Each section adds layers: bass, drums, strings. By the final chorus it becomes a massive anthemic wall of sound with layered group vocals and a shimmering, buzzing string tremolo that evokes actual cicada song. Joyful, not melancholy — a celebration of brevity.

**BPM:** 112
**Length:** 3:45
**Seed:** 1704 (Brood X's cycle: 17 seasons, 4 stages felt right)
