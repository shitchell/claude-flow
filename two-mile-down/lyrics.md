[Verse 1]
Come all you weary deckhands and lend an ear to me
(Heave away, me boys, heave away!)
In the year of seventy-seven we went down beneath the sea
(And we never saw the light of day!)
We were sent to look for warm rock and to bring the readings home
(Heave away, me boys, heave away!)
But we found a burning city on a chimney made of stone
(And we never saw the light of day!)

[Chorus]
So haul away — no sun required!
Haul away, boys, haul away!
There's a garden down in the dark and it never needed fire
from a star ninety-three million mile away!
Haul away — no sun required!
Haul away, boys, haul away!
We were taught you need the morning,
well, the morning was a liar,
and we never saw the light of day!

[Verse 2]
Oh the worms stood tall as sailors in a red and bloody row
(Heave away, me boys, heave away!)
With no mouth to do the eating and no eye to see below
(And we never saw the light of day!)
They drink the water's poison and they make a living still
(Heave away, me boys, heave away!)
There's a furnace underneath us, and it's running, and it will
(And we never saw the light of day!)

[Verse 3]
Now every vent goes quiet, aye, they always run down cold
(Heave away, me boys, heave away!)
And the whole town takes to drifting with no chart and nothing told
(And we never saw the light of day!)
But somebody always finds it, some poor beggar on the tide
(Heave away, me boys, heave away!)
Finds another burning chimney and he settles down beside
(And we never saw the light of day!)

[Final Chorus]
So haul away — no sun required!
Haul away, boys, haul away!
There's a garden down in the dark and it never needed fire
from a star ninety-three million mile away!
Haul away — no sun required!
Haul away, boys, haul away!
We were taught you need the morning,
well, the morning was a liar —
and we never saw the light of day!
