**Title:** *Two Mile Down (No Sun Required)*

**Summary:** the 1977 discovery of hydrothermal vent life retold as a stomping sea shanty

**Sound instructions:** Full-throated sea shanty, 4/4, stomping and joyful. Open cold with a single gruff solo male voice and boot-stomps on wooden deck — no instruments for the first two lines. Crew answers land as a raw unpolished group of 8-12 men, slightly out of tune with each other, recorded like they're all in one room. Instruments enter on the first chorus: concertina/melodeon carrying the melody, bodhran and stomps driving the pulse, upright bass, fiddle doubling up an octave, occasional tin whistle flourish between lines. Verse 2 add banjo. Keep it dry and live — minimal reverb, room-mic feel, breaths and shouts left in. Drop out to voices and stomps only at the top of the final chorus, then slam the full band back in for the last four lines. End on a ragged shouted unison, one loud stomp, done. No fade.
