## Eight Minutes

Title: Eight Minutes

BPM: 84

Length: 3:50

Seed: 299792458 (the speed of light, felt appropriate)

Sound instructions: Warm, spacious indie-folk with ambient electronic textures. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar and soft piano at the base, with slowly swelling analog synth pads that grow brighter as the song
progresses — the arrangement should feel like it's literally emerging from darkness into light. Gentle brushed drums enter at the second verse. Intimate, breathy vocals (any gender), close-mic'd, with wide
reverb-soaked harmonies on the choruses. The bridge should strip down to almost nothing — one voice, one held synth note — then the final chorus blooms wide open with shimmering high strings and layered vocals.
End on a single sustained warm chord that fades slowly, like dusk.

Lyrics:

(Verse 1)
Born in the crush of a burning heart,
a spark with nowhere to go —
every direction is a wall of gold,
a hundred thousand years of almost.

(Verse 2)
Nobody sees the wandering,
the collisions in the deep.
You were lost so long the mountains changed,
the ice came down and fell back to the sea.

(Chorus)
But the last eight minutes are a straight line —
falling through the dark, wearing all that time.
And you land like it was easy,
warm on someone's skin.
They will never know the maze you carried,
they just say — the sun is out again.

(Verse 3)
Somewhere a kid on a fire escape
tilts her face up, closes her eyes.
She's holding light that's older than the wheel,
older than every word for sky.

(Chorus)
'Cause the last eight minutes are a straight line —
falling through the dark, wearing all that time.
And you land like it was easy,
warm on someone's skin.
They will never know the maze you carried,
they just say — the sun is out again.

(Bridge)
Every ray is an escape story.
Every noon, a jailbreak glory.
So if your way is slow and blind —
so was all this light. So was all this light.

(Final Chorus)
And your last eight minutes are a straight line —
out of every dark, out of all that time.
And you'll land like it was easy,
like you always knew the way.
No one has to know the maze you carried —
just be someone's sunny day.
