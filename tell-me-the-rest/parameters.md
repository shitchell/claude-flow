**Title:** `Tell Me the Rest`

**Summary:** an all-night diner conversation — staying until someone's story is fully heard

**BPM:** `72`

**Length:** `3:40`

**Seed:** `306`

**Sound:**
> Slow indie-folk with a warm electronic underbelly. Brushed drums, upright bass, felted piano, a lone pedal steel drifting in late. Audible tape hiss and room tone throughout — it should sound recorded in an empty building at 4am. Vocal is close-mic'd and intimate, nearly spoken in the verses, cracking open into full chest voice on the chorus. Bridge stacks four or five vocal harmonies, then strips back to nothing. Final line delivered almost dry, no reverb. Think Big Thief, Sufjan Stevens, Bon Iver's *For Emma*.
