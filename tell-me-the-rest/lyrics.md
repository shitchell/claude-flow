# Tell Me the Rest

[Verse 1]
You shook the weather off your coat,
a paperback gone soft with rain.
Took the stool that nobody takes,
told me a name I'd never say again.

[Pre-Chorus]
And the coffee went cold in increments,
and the fry cook killed the radio,
and outside the whole town was a held breath —

[Chorus]
Tell me the rest. Tell me the rest.
I've got until the streetlights go.
I'm not saving this for later,
I'm just here, and I want to know.
Tell me the rest.

[Verse 2]
You said you left in a hurry —
left the light on, left the door.
Said your mother's hands in the garden,
said the dog's name twice, then didn't say more.

[Pre-Chorus]
And I didn't reach for a single thing,
and I didn't ask you to explain,
and the window went from black to gray —

[Chorus]
Tell me the rest. Tell me the rest.
I've got until the streetlights go.
I'm not saving this for later,
I'm just here, and I want to know.
Tell me the rest.

[Bridge]
Some things aren't built to be carried.
Some things are just built to be held.
The candle doesn't ask the dark
what it plans to do with all that light.
It just burns. It just burns. It just burns.

[Outro]
The sky went the color of a nickel.
You paid for us both. That was that.
I walked out into the morning
and I couldn't tell you what you said —

only that I meant every word back.
