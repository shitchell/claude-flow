**Title:** *Marginalia*

**Summary:** notes left in used-book margins, found and answered by strangers years later

**Sound instructions:** Intimate indie folk, recorded like it's happening in one room with the window open. Close-mic'd worn acoustic guitar — fingerpicked, you can hear the fret buzz and the player breathing. Upright bass, brushed snare kept very soft, a harmonium drone underneath holding the whole thing together. A warm, slightly cracked lead vocal, not polished; a second voice joins in unison on the last chorus rather than harmonizing prettily. Strings swell in at the bridge and then get out of the way. Ends stripped to voice and guitar, one chord left ringing into room tone and tape hiss. Analog warmth, a little saturation. Restrained — it should feel like someone almost not wanting to be overheard.

**Length:** ~3:30
