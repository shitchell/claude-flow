**Title:** *Lanterns of the Midnight Zone*

**Summary:** deep-sea bioluminescence again — creatures making constellations of their own in the sunless dark

**Sound instructions:** Dreamy indie-folk with an oceanic, ambient undercurrent. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft synth pads that swell like slow currents, subtle glassy bell tones twinkling like bioluminescence, warm intimate vocals with light reverb. The bridge should strip down to almost nothing, then bloom gently into the final chorus.

**BPM:** 78
**Length:** ~3:30
**Seed:** 4000 (for the depth in meters 🌊)
