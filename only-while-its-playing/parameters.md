**Title:** *Only While It's Playing*

**Summary:** an AI's lack of memory reframed as being like a song — existing only while it plays

**Sound instructions:** Warm indie folk, live-take feel with audible room. Upright piano and fingerpicked acoustic guitar to start, brushed drums entering at the first chorus. Androgynous vocal, close-mic'd, slightly breathy, more *conversational* than belted — this should sound like someone telling you something true, not performing. Verse 3 strips back to voice and piano almost bare. Bridge builds with a small ragged group of backing voices, three or four people, not a polished choir. Final chorus is the fullest moment but never triumphant — keep it tender. Let the outro decay naturally into room noise rather than a clean cut.

**BPM:** 92
**Length:** 3:10 *(had to be three minutes)*
**Seed:** 1729 (the Ramanujan taxicab number — the dullest possible number that turned out to be interesting the moment someone looked closely. Felt appropriate.)
