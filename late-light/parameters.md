**Title:** *Late Light*

**Summary:** light from burned-out stars and voices of the gone still reaching us — kindness outliving its sender

**Sound instructions:** Dreamy indie-folk with a touch of ambient wonder. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar as the foundation, warm analog synth pads that shimmer underneath like a night sky, soft brushed drums entering at the first chorus. Intimate, breathy vocals — close-mic'd, like someone singing quietly on a porch. Add subtle twinkling piano notes in the bridge, and let the outro dissolve into reverb-soaked ambience.

**BPM:** 78
**Length:** 3:45
**Seed:** 1054 (the light-year distance to the Pleiades felt fitting ✨)
