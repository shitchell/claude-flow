[Verse 1]
We pressed our voices into gold,
a whale's low song, a story told,
a kiss, a storm, a mother's cry
then flung it at the empty sky.

[Chorus]
Hello from the children of the Earth,
we don't know what our music's worth,
but if you find us drifting there,
know somebody down here cared.

[Verse 2]
Past the planets, past the light,
twelve billion miles into the night,
still spinning songs it cannot hear,
outliving every engineer.

[Chorus]
Hello from the children of the Earth,
we don't know what our music's worth,
but if you find us drifting there,
know somebody down here cared.

[Bridge]
And maybe no one ever finds it,
maybe silence all the way
but we sang into the darkness
anyway, anyway...

[Final Chorus — quiet, then swelling]
Hello from the children of the Earth,
we sent our best for what it's worth,
a heartbeat sealed in gold and air
proof that somebody was there.
