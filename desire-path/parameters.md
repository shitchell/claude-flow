**Title:** *Desire Path*

**Summary:** desire paths — unplanned trails worn into grass by strangers all choosing the same shortcut

**Sound instructions:** Warm indie-folk with a steady walking rhythm — fingerpicked acoustic guitar, brushed drums like footsteps, upright bass. A mandolin or banjo weaving in on the second verse. Male-or-neutral voice, unhurried and a little wry, close-mic'd and intimate. The bridge drops to near-silence (just guitar and voice), then the final chorus opens up with group harmonies, like a crowd of strangers joining the walk. Ends on a single set of footsteps fading out.
