**Title:** *Satellite Lullaby*

**Summary:** a decommissioned satellite still transmitting a lullaby nobody is listening for

**Sound instructions:** Dreamy synth-pop with a nostalgic, slightly melancholic warmth — think glassy analog synths, soft sidechain pulse, warm sub-bass, distant vocoder textures on the backing vocals, subtle vinyl crackle/static bursts panned around the stereo field. Female or androgynous lead vocal, breathy and intimate. Build from sparse and cold in the verses to lush and enveloping by the final chorus, like the satellite slowly waking up.

**BPM:** 88
**Length:** ~3:15
