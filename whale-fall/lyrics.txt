[Verse 1]
Forty years I carried song
across a hundred fathoms wide
now I'm falling, falling slow
no current left to fight the tide

Down where sunlight loses hope
down where no one keeps the time
I go dark to feed the dark
one long body, one last line

[Chorus]
Call it dying if you need to
I call it setting a table
Fifty years of feast beginning
where the light runs out of cable

Eat me slow, eat me whole
Let something else begin to grow
I was never meant to keep
I was always built to go

[Verse 2]
First the sharks, then hagfish come
then worms with roses for a mouth
bone-eating, bloom-white, blind
finding warmth in what runs out

Bacteria wake the sediment
a chemistry no sun has seen
they don't know I had a name
they just know I'm evergreen

[Chorus]
Call it dying if you need to
I call it setting a table
Fifty years of feast beginning
where the light runs out of cable

Eat me slow, eat me whole
Let something else begin to grow
I was never meant to keep
I was always built to go

[Bridge — soft, almost spoken, strings swelling underneath]
Somewhere up there they still sing
the songs I used to know
somewhere up there it's still bright
but I don't need it, down below

I am becoming reef
I am becoming home
a hundred small forevers
built from one big bone

[Final Chorus — full arrangement, key lifted]
Call it dying if you want to
I call it setting the table
Half a century of feasting
in the dark, so slowly, sung

Eat me slow, eat me whole
I became the ground you grow
Nothing wasted, nothing lost
just a long, long way to go

[Outro — whale-song sample returns, distant, answered now by new small voices: clicks, chitters, hum of bacteria mat, fading to silence]
