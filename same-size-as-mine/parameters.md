**Title:** *Same Size as Mine*

**Summary:** stenciled handprints in paleolithic caves — a hand made of bare stone where the paint didn't land, still matching the palm of anyone who visits

**Sound instructions:** Warm, intimate folk. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, soft brushed drums, a low cello underneath. A single genderless voice, close-mic'd like they're telling you a secret by a fire. Sparse first verse, builds gently — add harmonies on the final chorus like more voices joining across time. Ends quietly, almost a cappella.

**BPM:** 92
**Length:** ~3:30
**Seed:** "dealer's choice — but if you want one, try 10000 (years, give or take) 🙂"
