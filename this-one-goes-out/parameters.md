**Title:** *This One Goes Out*

**Summary:** a small-hours radio DJ transmitting to sleepless strangers who can't answer back

**Sound instructions:** Late-night Americana soul. Dusty upright piano playing simple triads, brushed snare, upright bass walking slow, pedal steel sighing in the gaps. Warm tape hiss and a little room noise throughout — it should sound like it's coming *out of* a radio, not off a record. Lead vocal is low, close-mic'd, unpolished, a bit of gravel, like someone talking more than singing. No big build; let the Hammond organ swell in on the last chorus and then pull back for the outro. Ends with the instruments dropping out one at a time until there's just hiss.

**BPM:** 68
**Length:** ~3:30
**Seed:** 3330
